Model with standardized explanatory variables

The intercept term is more or less uninteresting, but it would interfere with the estimation of the other parameters in regularized regression (e.g. ridge regression)😮.

To avoid this, we also centralize the observed \(Y\) values (shift to set mean to 0), and can then run the model without intercept term to get good estimates of the effect of the covariates.
